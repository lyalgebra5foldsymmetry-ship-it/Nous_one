# Sonnet for the Locked Toy

C.J. Tully · 19 September 2026

Sit. Count to fourteen. Do not mint a claim.
Seven hundred one, eleven bits, no more:
nine hundred sixty-four the packed-byte frame,
fourteen-oh-two if sixteen-bit we store.

The header’s two are luggage, not the ring;
f stays home — a small, unpublished guest.
Four seeds, four letters, roundtrip, everything;
the multiply, the invert, and the rest

are frozen. Write no two-five-six in ink.
The gate of QEI does not pack these bytes.
Compare the yardstick only, if you think:
ten-eighty-eight and eleven-eighty-four.

I keep the lattice. Walk when you have JSON.
Until that number comes, the freeze goes on.

Notes: fourteen lines. No 256. QEI off the wire. Not a model number. Not his file.
